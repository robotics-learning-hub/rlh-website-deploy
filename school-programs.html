School STEM Programs

Hands-on STEM Programs for Schools

RLH works with schools to plan practical robotics, coding, electronics, and AI learning activities for students. Program scope, schedule, materials, fees, and responsibilities are discussed and agreed with each school before delivery.

Teacher capacity-building workshops are a separate service and are included only when specifically requested and agreed.

School Program Approach

A program shaped around your students and school context.

We discuss learner age, learning goals, timetable, facilities, materials, and budget with the school contact. RLH then shares a proposed scope for review. Dates, fees, delivery arrangements, and each party’s responsibilities are confirmed before the program begins.

Core School Services

Student learning options for schools.

Robotics, Coding & Electronics

Hands-on student activities using age-appropriate builds and concepts, with the topics and level agreed during planning.

School STEM Clubs

Club sessions can be arranged around the school calendar. Frequency, session length, projects, and group size are set out in the agreed plan.

Holiday Programs

Schools may enquire about holiday learning activities. Dates, format, places, and fees depend on availability and are confirmed in advance.

Kits & Materials

Materials can be discussed against the planned activities. Any supply, use, or school-provided equipment is specified in the program scope.

Student Project Sharing

Project presentations or showcases can be discussed as part of planning and included when agreed in the program scope.

How Schools Start With RLH

Agree the plan before sessions begin.

A short planning conversation helps both sides establish whether the proposed activities fit the school’s goals, calendar, and available resources.

1

Consultation

We identify learner levels, school priorities, timetable, and available facilities.

2

Written Scope

The proposed activities, schedule, fees, materials, and responsibilities are shared for the school’s review.

3

Confirm Arrangements

Delivery begins after both sides confirm the scope, dates, logistics, and payment terms.

4

Student Activities

RLH delivers the agreed student sessions. Any additional workshop or showcase is planned separately or clearly included in the scope.

Learning Experience
