Learning Model

Flexible, structured, and practical from week one.

Online learners attend two sessions per week. Younger learners take 1-1.5 hour sessions, while older learners take 2 hour practical sessions. Physical cohorts run on weekends with Saturday and optional Sunday practical sessions.

Online Academy

Live guided classes, project assignments, parent updates, and mentor support.

Physical Cohorts

Hands-on labs, kit-based building, peer collaboration, and practical demonstrations.

Capstone Showcase

Every pathway ends with a project learners can present, improve, and add to a portfolio.
