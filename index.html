Welcome to Robotics Learning Hub

Robotics Learning Hub (RLH) equips learners with practical technology skills through hands-on training, school partnerships, weekend/online programs, community outreaches and STEM learning kits. Our approach is simple: empower young innovators to learn by building, coding, testing, and creating with confidence.
