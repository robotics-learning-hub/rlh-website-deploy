About Robotics Learning Hub

Practical STEM learning for young innovators.

Robotics Learning Hub (RLH) is a Kampala-based STEM learning and innovation organization. We help young people understand technology by building, testing and improving practical projects.

Who we are

We make room for curiosity, making and problem-solving.

RLH works with children and young people, parents, schools and educators to make robotics and other technology skills hands-on and approachable.

Our learning brings together guided activities, useful tools and materials, and projects that give learners a chance to put ideas into practice. We focus on the process: asking questions, trying a design, seeing what happens, and making it better.

Home STEM Coaching is available for learners aged 5–18+. RLH also offers school programs, learning pathways, and kits and educational materials.

Our learning approach

Learning is something learners do.

We encourage learners to build understanding through practical work and reflection.

Start with a question

Turn curiosity into an idea, design or challenge to explore.

Make and experiment

Use suitable tools and materials to bring ideas into a practical activity.

Test and improve

Notice what works, debug what does not, and make thoughtful changes.

Explain the learning

Give learners opportunities to describe their project and the ideas behind it.

Rooted in Kampala

A local learning hub with practical ways to take part.

Robotics Learning Hub is based in Kampala, Uganda, and serves families and schools through available learning arrangements.

Home visits and other formats depend on location, scheduling and availability. RLH discusses the appropriate option, session details and equipment with families before a program is confirmed.

For Home Coaching, sessions are 1–2 hours and fees start from UGX 30,000. Contact RLH to ask about your learner's interests and the current arrangements.
