About RLH

Future Skills Academy for Young Innovators

Robotics Learning Hub (RLH) equips learners, schools, and communities with practical STEM skills through robotics, AI, coding, IoT, electronics, web development, 3D design, and hands-on learning kits.

Who We Are

We help young people learn by building what they can imagine.

RLH is a STEM education and innovation organization based in Kampala, Uganda. We support schools, parents, communities, and young innovators with practical future skills learning for learners aged 6-18+.

Our work combines structured pathways, hands-on kits, guided mentorship, project showcases, and school STEM programs that turn technology from theory into confidence.

Our Mission

To inspire future-ready innovators by equipping learners with practical technology and innovation skills through hands-on programs aligned with real-world problem solving.

Our Vision

To be Africa's leading hub for practical STEM learning and real-world innovation development for young learners and schools.

What Makes RLH Different

Practical learning with visible outcomes.

Hands-On Learning

Students build, test, troubleshoot, and present real robotics and digital projects.

School Program Focus

We support schools with clubs, labs, exhibitions, teacher training, and curriculum support.

Future Skills Breadth

RLH goes beyond robotics into AI, coding, web, IoT, electronics, FreeCAD, and 3D design.

Build future-ready learners with RLH.

We are ready to support parents, schools, clubs, and young innovators.
